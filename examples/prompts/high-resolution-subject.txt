v0.3 pattern-draft migration
============================

The 58 × 58 semantic pattern draft is a lossless
four-raster-pixels-per-cell rendering of the previously committed public
compiler matrix. It was created locally and deterministically; no user image or
image-generation call was involved. The v0.3 high-resolution route keeps the
original illustration as `--input` and compiles the separate semantic draft
through `--draft-input`.
